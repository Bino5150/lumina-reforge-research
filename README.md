**Abstract in brief**
Lumina Reforge is an owner-triggered transactional reconstruction mechanism that replaces an execution-heavy active workbench with a bounded context drawn from an unchanged durable conversation and a provenance-aware continuity checkpoint. Exact checkpoint and state bindings, machine-grounded facts, and transactional guards prevent a stale or invalid rebuild from replacing live working state.
In two retrospectively documented operational cases, estimated **total active context** fell by  **62.2% and 76.6%**; a separate shakedown measured an  **82.8% reduction of estimated live-history tokens** (a different denominator). The durable conversation was unchanged. A blind continuity probe correctly recovered durable identifiers and declined to invent discarded telemetry, but  **missed one task-critical exact ephemeral detail**. No lossless-fidelity, comparative-superiority, or measured billing/latency claim is made.
